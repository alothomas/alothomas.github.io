About

I am a PhD student in Computational Biology at the University of Bern, working in the Luisier Lab with Raphaëlle Luisier and Cédric Vincent-Cuaz as part of the SATURNA program.

Research

My research focuses on graph representation learning for context-specific molecular systems. I work on context-aware protein and RNA interaction networks, combining graph neural networks, biological foundation models, multimodal molecular data, and self-supervised learning.
